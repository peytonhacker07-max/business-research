Why Angle Stop Plumbing Partnered with Cala Heat Pump Water Systems

By Jim Nephew · August 28, 2026

At Angle Stop Plumbing, we are always looking for better ways to serve homeowners with solutions that combine comfort, efficiency, and long-term value. That is why we are excited to announce our new partnership with Cala Heat Pump Water Systems - an innovative water heating solution designed to help homeowners lower energy costs without sacrificing performance.

This partnership brings together two important advantages: best-in-class water heating technology and best-in-class service. For our customers, that means access to a smarter, more energy-efficient water heater installed and supported by a team they trust.

A Smarter Way to Heat Water

Traditional water heaters use energy whenever they need to maintain hot water, regardless of when electricity costs are highest. Cala takes a more intelligent approach.

Cala Heat Pump Water Systems are designed to utilize energy at the lowest price points during a 24-hour period. By adjusting operation based on energy pricing, the system helps reduce overall consumer cost while still delivering reliable hot water when it is needed most.

For homeowners, that means:

  • Lower utility costs through smarter energy usage
  • Greater energy efficiency compared to conventional systems
  • Reliable hot water performance for daily household needs
  • A more future-ready home with advanced plumbing technology

This is not just a new water heater. It is a more strategic and cost-conscious approach to home energy use.

Why This Matters for Homeowners

Rising utility costs have made energy efficiency more important than ever. Homeowners are looking for products that do more than work - they want systems that work smarter.

That is where this partnership stands out. Cala's intelligent heat pump water heating technology helps homeowners take advantage of lower energy rates throughout the day, while Angle Stop Plumbing ensures every installation is handled with expert care and dependable service.

The result is a complete solution that offers both innovation and peace of mind.

Best-in-Class Product Meets Best-in-Class Service

A high-performance system is only as good as the team installing and servicing it. At Angle Stop Plumbing, we believe customers deserve both exceptional products and exceptional support.

With this new partnership, customers can expect:

  • Expert guidance in choosing the right water heating solution
  • Professional installation from a trusted plumbing team
  • Dependable service and support after installation
  • Confidence in a premium product backed by experienced professionals

Our goal is simple: deliver solutions that help customers save money, improve efficiency, and feel confident in their investment.

Looking Ahead

The partnership between Angle Stop Plumbing and Cala Heat Pump Water Systems represents an exciting step forward in home water heating. By combining smart energy optimization with trusted local service, we are making it easier for homeowners to upgrade to a system that supports both comfort and savings.

If you are considering a new water heater, this partnership offers a powerful opportunity to invest in a solution that is efficient, innovative, and backed by a team committed to doing the job right.

For homeowners who want lower operating costs and high-quality service, Angle Stop Plumbing and Cala Heat Pump Water Systems are a smart fit for the future of home comfort.
