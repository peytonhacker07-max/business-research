## What Is a Water Softener?  {bold}

A water softener is a system designed to reduce the minerals that cause hard water, primarily calcium and magnesium. These minerals can build up inside pipes, fixtures, water heaters, and appliances over time, leading to reduced performance and increased wear.

By treating water before it moves through your plumbing system, a water softener can help minimize scale buildup and improve how water feels and performs throughout your home.

## Signs You May Need a Water Softener  {bold}

You may benefit from a water softener system if you notice any of the following:

- White scale buildup on faucets and showerheads

- Soap scum on sinks, tubs, or shower walls

- Spots or film on dishes and glassware

- Dry skin or dull hair after bathing

- Stiff or dingy laundry

- Reduced water heater efficiency

- Mineral buildup in plumbing fixtures and appliances

- Hard water stains around drains and faucets

- Difficulty getting soap to lather well

These are common signs that hard water minerals are affecting your plumbing system and daily water use.
