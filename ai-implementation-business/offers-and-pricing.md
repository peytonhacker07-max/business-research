# Offers & Pricing

Three productized offers. Every client conversation should end at one of
these. Fixed scope, fixed price — never hourly, never custom quotes.

---

## Offer 1: "Get Online" — website + Google presence
**The pitch:** "Customers Google you and find nothing. I'll fix that this week."

What they get:
- A clean 1–3 page website (services, hours, photos, phone, map, reviews)
- Google Business Profile claimed, verified, and fully filled out
- A booking link (free tier of Square Appointments / Booksy / Calendly)
- Their site connected to a domain they own

**Price: $500 setup + $49/mo** (hosting, edits, keeping hours/photos current)

Your cost: ~$12/yr domain + free static hosting (GitHub Pages/Cloudflare
Pages, which you already know) + 3–5 hours of your time with AI doing the
heavy lifting. Margin on setup is basically all of it.

---

## Offer 2: "Never Miss a Customer" — missed-call text-back + AI receptionist
**The pitch:** "You're cutting hair / under a car and can't answer the phone.
Every missed call gets an instant text: 'Sorry we missed you — want to book?'
with your booking link. Nobody goes to your competitor."

What they get:
- Missed-call auto text-back with their booking link
- An AI-powered SMS assistant that can answer basics (hours, prices,
  location) and take booking requests
- A simple weekly summary: calls missed, texts sent, bookings recovered

**Price: $150–200/mo** (no setup fee if they take Offer 1 too)

Your cost: a service like OpenPhone/Podium-style tools or a Twilio number +
an LLM API — roughly $20–50/mo per client. This is your recurring-revenue
engine. Ten clients on this = $1,500–2,000/mo.

**This is the highest-value offer for your list.** Barbershops and auto
shops miss calls constantly, and each missed call is a $30–100 customer.
The math sells itself: "If this saves you two customers a month, it's paid
for itself."

---

## Offer 3: "Reputation Engine" — reviews on autopilot
**The pitch:** "Your reviews are why people pick you. Let's get more of them,
automatically, and never leave one unanswered."

What they get:
- Automatic post-visit text/QR asking happy customers for a Google review
- AI-drafted responses to every new review (they approve, you post)
- Monthly report: new reviews, rating trend

**Price: $99/mo**

Your cost: near zero — a QR code, a review link, and an LLM drafting
responses. Great add-on; rarely the lead offer.

---

## The bundle

**"Full Setup": all three for $500 setup + $249/mo.** Anchor high: present
the bundle first, let them pick pieces. A client on the full bundle is worth
~$3,500 in year one.

## First-clients discount (use sparingly)

"I'm launching and picking 3 businesses in [town] to get my founding rate —
$250 setup instead of $500, price locked for a year, in exchange for a
Google review and permission to use you as a case study." Say this only when
it's true, and stop at 3.

## Pricing rules

1. Never quote a price before you've named the problem in their own numbers
   ("how many calls do you miss a day?").
2. Monthly fees are the business. Setup fees pay this month's bills;
   recurring pays next year's.
3. Raise prices every ~5 clients. Client #6 pays more than client #1 —
   you're better and you have proof.
4. Get paid before you build: setup fee (or half) up front, always.
