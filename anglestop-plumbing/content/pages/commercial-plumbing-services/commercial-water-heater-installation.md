## Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Commercial Water Heater  {bold}

If your current system is struggling to keep up, it may be time for a replacement. Common signs include:

- Inconsistent or insufficient hot water

- Rising energy bills

- Rust-colored water

- Strange noises from the unit

- Water leaks around the tank

- Frequent repairs

- Aging equipment nearing the end of its service life

Replacing an older water heater before it fails completely can help your business avoid interruptions and emergency repair costs.
