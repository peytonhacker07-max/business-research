Here are the top 10 causes — drawn from common local issues — and how proactive inspections make all the difference.

## 1. Burst or Leaking Pipes  {bold}

Freezing temperatures or high-pressure cause pipes to crack, especially in uninsulated attics or crawlspaces. A small pinhole can release gallons before you notice. <<http://notice.hannahrestoration.com>>

Inspection fix: Technicians use infrared cameras to spot hidden weaknesses during routine checks.

## 2. Faulty Appliances (Washers, Dishwashers)  {bold}

Worn hoses or clogs lead to overflows, flooding floors and cabinets. <<http://cabinets.hannahrestoration.com>>

Inspection fix: Angle Stop tests connections and replaces at-risk parts before failure.

## 3. Water Heater Corrosion and Leaks  {bold}

Rust buildup (affecting 45% of homes) reduces efficiency and causes tanks to fail, spilling water everywhere. <<http://everywhere.anglestopplumbing.com>>

Inspection fix: Camera inspections inside the tank reveal corrosion early; pair with a whole-house filter to prevent it.

## 4. Roof Leaks and Gutters  {bold}

Clogged gutters overflow, seeping into foundations or interiors during storms. <<http://storms.hannahrestoration.com>>

Inspection fix: Exterior checks identify drainage issues tied to plumbing vulnerabilities.

## 5. Sump Pump Failures  {bold}

Power outages or clogs flood basements in flood-prone Middle Tennessee. <<http://Tennessee.hannahrestoration.com>>

Inspection fix: Annual testing ensures pumps work; add backups for reliability.

## 6. Sewer Backups  {bold}

Roots, blockages, or heavy rain push contaminated water indoors. <<http://indoors.hannahrestoration.com>>

Inspection fix: Camera scoping clears lines and spots impending blockages.

## 7. Water Hammer  {bold}

Sudden flow stops create shockwaves, loosening pipes and fittings over time. <<http://time.anglestopplumbing.com>>

Inspection fix: Install arrestors and regulate pressure during service visits.

## 8. Backflow Preventer Malfunctions  {bold}

Worn seals allow contamination and trigger utility shutoffs if untested. <<http://untested.anglestopplumbing.com>>

Inspection fix: Certified annual testing keeps you compliant and safe.

## 9. Outdoor Faucet Leaks  {bold}

Drips waste water and signal internal issues, worsening in cold weather. <<http://weather.anglestopplumbing.com>>

Inspection fix: Winterization and repairs prevent expansion cracks.

## 10. Corrosion in Fixtures and Pipes  {bold}

Minerals erode faucets, showers, and lines, restricting flow and causing pinhole leaks — especially in crawlspace homes. <<http://homes.anglestopplumbing.com>>

Inspection fix: Water quality tests and filtration systems halt corrosion at the source.
