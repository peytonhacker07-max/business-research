# Delivery Checklists

Same steps every client = consistent quality, fast delivery, and you can
eventually hand this to someone else. Speed is your reputation: quote
"live within a week," deliver in 2–3 days.

---

## Kickoff (every client, 30 min, do it on the spot if possible)

- [ ] Get paid: setup fee (or half) via Stripe/Square invoice
- [ ] Collect: business name as they want it spelled, hours, services +
      prices, phone, address, 10–20 photos (shoot them yourself on your
      phone — 15 minutes, huge quality boost), anything they hate/love
      about competitors' sites
- [ ] Get access: Google Business Profile (add yourself as manager),
      domain if they have one
- [ ] Set expectations in writing (text is fine): what they get, when it's
      live, what the monthly fee covers, 1 round of revisions on launch

## Offer 1: Get Online

- [ ] Buy/point domain (they own it — registered in THEIR name/account,
      you as technical contact; never hold a client's domain hostage)
- [ ] Build the site with AI assist; static hosting (GitHub Pages /
      Cloudflare Pages). Must-haves: click-to-call phone number, hours,
      services + prices, embedded map, 3–5 best Google reviews quoted,
      booking button, photos
- [ ] Mobile check — 90% of their traffic is phones
- [ ] Google Business Profile: claim/verify, every field filled, photos
      uploaded, website + booking links added
- [ ] Set up free booking tool (Square Appointments / Booksy) with their
      real services and hours; connect it to the site's Book button
- [ ] Walk the owner through it in person or on a call (10 min)
- [ ] Launch text to client + ask: "Anything you'd change?" (1 revision
      round)

## Offer 2: Never Miss a Customer

- [ ] Set up business number (port or forward their existing line —
      forward is easier: their phone rings normally, missed calls hit
      your system)
- [ ] Configure missed-call auto-text with their booking link, written in
      their voice ("Sorry we missed you! Want to grab a spot? Book here: …")
- [ ] Set up AI SMS assistant with a knowledge doc: hours, prices,
      services, location, parking, cancellation policy. Hard rule in the
      prompt: if unsure, say "let me have [owner] text you back" — never
      guess prices or promise appointments the calendar can't see
- [ ] Test it yourself 10+ ways (wrong questions, slang, Spanish if
      relevant) before it goes live
- [ ] Weekly summary set up (even manual at first: 5 min, screenshot +
      3 numbers texted to the owner — "12 missed calls, 12 texts sent,
      4 booked")

## Offer 3: Reputation Engine

- [ ] Generate their Google review link + QR code; print a small counter
      card ("Loved your cut? 30 seconds: leave us a review") — get it
      printed nicely, it sits next to their register forever
- [ ] If they have customer phone numbers: post-visit review-request text
- [ ] AI-drafted review responses: you draft with an LLM, owner approves
      via text, you post. Never auto-post without approval
- [ ] Monthly: one text to owner with review count, rating, best new review

---

## Monthly retainer routine (per client, ~30–45 min/mo)

- [ ] Update site: hours changes, new photos, price changes
- [ ] Post 1–2 photos to Google Business Profile (keeps the listing "active")
- [ ] Check booking tool + phone system still working (test one booking)
- [ ] Respond to new reviews
- [ ] Send the monthly text report — 3 numbers + one sentence. This text
      is why they keep paying you. Never skip it.

## Rules that keep clients

1. Answer their texts same-day, even just "on it."
2. Everything is in their name (domain, Google profile, booking account) —
   you're the manager, not the owner. This builds trust and it's ethical.
3. When something breaks, tell them before they notice.
4. Every deliverable ends with a question: "anything you'd change?"
