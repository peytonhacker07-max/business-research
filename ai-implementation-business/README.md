# AI Implementation Business — Starter Kit

This folder is the playbook for turning your skills + your lead list
(`../tennessee_businesses_no_website.csv`) into a paying business.

## The honest framing

The money is NOT in "selling AI." Local business owners don't wake up wanting
AI — they wake up annoyed that they missed 6 calls while cutting hair, that
customers can't book online, and that they have no website while their
competitor does. AI is just the cheapest, fastest way for YOU to fix those
problems. You sell the fixed problem, and AI is your margin.

Your unfair advantages right now:
1. You already have a researched list of ~100 TN businesses with good reviews
   and no website — that research alone is worth money.
2. You've already shipped websites (MyHomeschoolHelp in this repo) — you're
   not starting from zero.
3. You're local. A kid from Jackson/Memphis walking into a barbershop beats
   a cold email from an agency in another state every single time.

## The business in one sentence

**"I get your business online and stop you from losing customers to missed
calls — done for you, for a flat monthly fee."**

## Your first 30 days

**Week 1 — Get sellable**
- [ ] Pick a business name and set up the basics (see business-basics.md)
- [ ] Build ONE demo: pick a real barbershop from your list and build them a
      sample one-page site + booking link in an afternoon. This is your pitch.
- [ ] Set up a way to take payment (Stripe or Square invoices)
- [ ] Practice the walk-in pitch out loud 10 times (outreach-playbook.md)

**Week 2 — First 20 conversations**
- [ ] Sort your lead list: barbershops first (45 of them, and they're the
      easiest sale — they live off appointments)
- [ ] Visit or call 5 per day. Goal is conversations, not sales.
- [ ] Track every contact in a spreadsheet: name, date, response, next step

**Week 3–4 — Close and deliver**
- [ ] Goal: 2–3 paying clients at starter pricing
- [ ] Deliver fast (48–72 hours) — speed is your reputation
- [ ] Ask every happy client: "Who else do you know that needs this?" and
      for a Google review of YOUR business

**Realistic math:** 100 leads → ~30 real conversations → 3–5 clients in month
one is a genuinely good result. At $500 setup + $99/mo each, that's
$1,500–2,500 up front and $300–500/mo recurring — and month two is easier
because you have proof.

## What NOT to do

- Don't build a fancy website/brand for yourself first. One demo + one
  paying client beats a logo.
- Don't offer 10 services. Three, max. (See offers-and-pricing.md.)
- Don't promise "AI will grow your revenue 10x." Promise specific things:
  "you'll have a website this week" and "missed calls get a text back in
  10 seconds." Under-promise, over-deliver.
- Don't undercharge to $0. Free clients are the most demanding and give
  the worst referrals. Discount your first 2–3, never free.
