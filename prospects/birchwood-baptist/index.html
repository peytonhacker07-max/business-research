New here?

Here's what a week at Birchwood looks like.

Whether it's your first Sunday or your five-hundredth, there's a seat for you.

10:00

Sunday School

Start your Sunday in a small class, open the Bible together, and get to know people before the service.

11:00

Worship + Message

Our main gathering: singing, prayer, and preaching from God's Word. We meet again Sunday evening at 6:00.

Wed

KidzLife & Youth

Kids have KidzLife from 6:00 to 7:30 and students meet for Youth from 6:30 to 7:30, while adults gather for the 6:30 message.

Have a question first? Call us at (423) 961-2525 or message us on Facebook.

Our story

Rooted in Birchwood for over 150 years.

The original church still stands at the center of the building we worship in today.

1873

Organized on the fourth Saturday in September by families from the Old Salem Baptist Church.

1879

The first church building is raised on land given by Malinda Erwin for the church and cemetery.

1904

The first addition is built, and later additions are each made to match the original structure.
