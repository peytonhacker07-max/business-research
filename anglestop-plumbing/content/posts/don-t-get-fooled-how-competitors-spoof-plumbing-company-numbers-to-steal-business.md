#### What’s Going On?

Phone number spoofing is when someone manipulates caller ID to make it look like the call is coming from a trusted source — like (615) 398-3048, our official number. You might think you’re talking with Angle Stop Plumbing, but it could actually be a different company trying to lure you in.

We know how confusing this can be, especially when you’re just trying to get your plumbing problem fixed quickly and safely.

#### Why Do Competitors Do This?

Unfortunately, some unethical businesses use this tactic to:

- Trick you into thinking you’re speaking with us.

- Offer “too good to be true” deals to win your business.

- Collect your personal details under false pretenses.

That’s not how we do business — and we want to help you avoid being misled!

#### How to Make Sure You’re Talking to the Real Angle Stop Plumbing

Before you book any plumbing service, take a moment to confirm who’s really on the line.

Here’s how:

- Ask for the full company name. Always ask, “Can you confirm your company name and location?” The real Angle Stop Plumbing team will always identify ourselves proudly and clearly.

- Hang up and call back. If something seems off, just hang up and call our verified number: (615) 398-3048 or visit [[www.AngleStopPlumbing.com|http://www.AngleStopPlumbing.com]] to confirm it’s us.

- Ask about your account or last service. If you’ve worked with us before, we’ll be able to confirm details from your last appointment — a scammer won’t.

- Ask for the technician’s name and ID info. Our technicians will always be transparent about who they are and what job they’re heading to.
