# What Is Water Hammer?

If you’ve ever heard a loud banging or knocking noise coming from your pipes when you turn off a faucet or your washing machine stops filling, you’ve experienced something called “water hammer.” This unsettling sound is more than just an annoyance—it’s a sign of a plumbing issue that, if left untreated, can lead to serious damage in your home.

![](wix:e41951d3621b4933a818a964fdbf116a.jpg)

## What Causes Water Hammer?

Water hammer occurs when the flow of water in your pipes is suddenly stopped or changed direction, creating a shockwave that reverberates through your plumbing system. This usually happens when a valve closes quickly, such as when a dishwasher or washing machine finishes filling. The shockwave causes the pipes to bang against each other or nearby surfaces, resulting in the characteristic hammering sound.

Common causes of water hammer include:

· High water pressure

· Loose pipe fittings or unsecured pipes

· Faulty or worn-out valves

· Lack of water hammer arrestors (devices designed to absorb the shock)

## Why Is Water Hammer Harmful?

While water hammer may seem like a minor inconvenience, it can actually be very harmful to your plumbing system over time. The repeated shockwaves can cause:

· Pipe Damage: Pipes may loosen, crack, or even burst due to ongoing stress.

· Fitting Failure: Joints and fittings can weaken, leading to leaks.

· Water Leaks: Leaks can cause significant water damage to walls, floors, and personal belongings.

· Noise Pollution: Persistent banging can disrupt the peace in your home.

Ignoring water hammer can ultimately result in expensive repairs, mold growth, and structural damage from water leaks. It’s always best to address the issue as soon as possible to protect your home and your wallet.
