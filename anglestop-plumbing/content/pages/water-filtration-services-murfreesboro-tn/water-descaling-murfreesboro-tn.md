## What Is a Water Descaling System?  {bold}

A water descaling system is a whole-home water treatment solution designed to reduce the effects of hard water scale on plumbing surfaces. Rather than removing hardness minerals like a traditional water softener, a descaling system helps condition the water in a way that reduces the tendency of those minerals to form hard scale deposits inside pipes, fixtures, and appliances.

These systems are commonly installed at the point where water enters the home, helping protect the plumbing system as water flows throughout the property.

For homeowners who want an alternative to salt-based water softeners, a whole-house water descaler can be an appealing option.
