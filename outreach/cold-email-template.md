## Notes

- Keep it short — these are busy shop owners reading on their phone between
  customers. Two screens max.
- Lead with THEIR reviews, not your pitch — it proves you actually looked
  at their business instead of mass-blasting a template.
- The portfolio link should point at a live, working example. Swap it for
  your best site once you have more than one.
- Track sends/replies in a simple sheet (business name, date emailed, date
  followed up, reply Y/N) so nothing falls through the cracks.
