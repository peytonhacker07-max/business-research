# Birchwood Baptist Church: homepage concept

Sample homepage redesign for birchwoodbaptistchurch.com (5436 TN-60, Birchwood, TN 37308 · (423) 961-2525).
Open `index.html` in a browser. Show `before-after-mobile.jpg` on your phone in person.

## What's wrong with their current site (checked Nov 2026)
- **Phone view is broken:** the homepage ends after the job posting. The address never appears, and the phone number is only behind an unlabeled blue "…" button.
- **Desktop:** the menu shows twice, "Homeschool Co-op" gets cut off at the edge, and there's a big empty navy block at the bottom.
- **Church name is white on beige:** ~1.6:1 contrast, below the 3:1 minimum for large text.
- **Leftovers from a DIY Wix build (©2020):** the co-op page lives at `/general-4`, there's a hidden "New Private Page", and an archived COVID-19 page, Events and VBS pages are hidden.
- **No online giving, map, email, "Plan a visit", or search description.** Browser tabs read "Birchwood Baptist Ch".
- **They're on a paid Wix plan**, so they already pay monthly.

## About the concept
- Every fact comes from their current site. The welcome lines are new copy.
- **Verify before going live:** co-op class groups (1st–2nd, 3rd–5th, 6th–12th) were read from garbled page text.
- **Placeholders to replace:** the "JH" circle needs a photo of the pastor. The church photo is theirs, with a new sky added, and it's soft because their original is low-resolution. A fresh photo would help a lot.
- **Before it goes live:** the "Design concept" banner and the `noindex` tag are there so this never gets mistaken for the real site. Remove both at launch.
- The map embed is from Google Maps and was not visually verified (blocked from the build environment). It has an "Open in Google Maps" fallback.

## Upsells
Online giving, an events/VBS page, a staff page, a co-op sign-up form, and a new photo of the building and pastor.
