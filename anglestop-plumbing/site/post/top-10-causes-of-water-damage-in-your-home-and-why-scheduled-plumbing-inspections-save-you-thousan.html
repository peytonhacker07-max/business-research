Top 10 Causes of Water Damage in Your Home — And Why Scheduled Plumbing Inspections Save You Thousands

By Jim Nephew · April 25, 2026

Water damage strikes without warning, turning minor issues into major disasters. In Middle Tennessee, where homes often sit on crawlspaces vulnerable to moisture and temperature swings, unchecked plumbing problems can lead to leaks, mold, and repair bills averaging $10,000 or more. The good news? Scheduled plumbing inspections from Angle Stop Plumbing catch these risks early, preventing costly claims and keeping your home safe.

Here are the top 10 causes — drawn from common local issues — and how proactive inspections make all the difference.

1. Burst or Leaking Pipes

Freezing temperatures or high-pressure cause pipes to crack, especially in uninsulated attics or crawlspaces. A small pinhole can release gallons before you notice.

Inspection fix: Technicians use infrared cameras to spot hidden weaknesses during routine checks.

2. Faulty Appliances (Washers, Dishwashers)

Worn hoses or clogs lead to overflows, flooding floors and cabinets.

Inspection fix: Angle Stop tests connections and replaces at-risk parts before failure.

3. Water Heater Corrosion and Leaks

Rust buildup (affecting 45% of homes) reduces efficiency and causes tanks to fail, spilling water everywhere.

Inspection fix: Camera inspections inside the tank reveal corrosion early; pair with a whole-house filter to prevent it.

4. Roof Leaks and Gutters

Clogged gutters overflow, seeping into foundations or interiors during storms.

Inspection fix: Exterior checks identify drainage issues tied to plumbing vulnerabilities.

5. Sump Pump Failures

Power outages or clogs flood basements in flood-prone Middle Tennessee.

Inspection fix: Annual testing ensures pumps work; add backups for reliability.

6. Sewer Backups

Roots, blockages, or heavy rain push contaminated water indoors.

Inspection fix: Camera scoping clears lines and spots impending blockages.

7. Water Hammer

Sudden flow stops create shockwaves, loosening pipes and fittings over time.

Inspection fix: Install arrestors and regulate pressure during service visits.

8. Backflow Preventer Malfunctions

Worn seals allow contamination and trigger utility shutoffs if untested.

Inspection fix: Certified annual testing keeps you compliant and safe.

9. Outdoor Faucet Leaks

Drips waste water and signal internal issues, worsening in cold weather.

Inspection fix: Winterization and repairs prevent expansion cracks.

10. Corrosion in Fixtures and Pipes

Minerals erode faucets, showers, and lines, restricting flow and causing pinhole leaks — especially in crawlspace homes.

Inspection fix: Water quality tests and filtration systems halt corrosion at the source.
