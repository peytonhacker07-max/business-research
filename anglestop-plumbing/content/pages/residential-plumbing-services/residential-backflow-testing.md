## What Is Backflow?  {bold}

Backflow occurs when water flows in the opposite direction from where it is supposed to go. This can happen due to a sudden drop in water pressure, known as backsiphonage, or from increased downstream pressure, known as backpressure.

When that happens, contaminants from irrigation systems, fire sprinklers, boilers, garden hoses, industrial equipment, or other cross-connections can enter the clean water supply. That is why a functioning backflow prevention device is so important.

Backflow testing helps verify that your assembly is operating properly and can prevent contaminated water from entering your potable water system.
