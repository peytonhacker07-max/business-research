# Outreach

- `lead-emails-candidates.csv` — best-effort email addresses found for the 100
  leads in `../tennessee_businesses_no_website.csv`.

## Read this before sending from lead-emails-candidates.csv

The research agent's sandbox couldn't load actual web pages (Facebook, Yelp,
BBB, directories, and individual business sites all blocked outbound
requests). Every email in this file came from a search engine's synthesized
snippets, not a page anyone actually looked at — and that method is known to
sometimes invent plausible-but-fake addresses (e.g. `firstname.lastname@
businessdomain.com` guesses). The agent caught and dropped a handful of those,
but more may have slipped through, **including some marked "High" confidence**
(that label assumed a page was viewed directly, which wasn't actually
possible here — so don't take "High" as verified).

**Before emailing any address in this file:** do a 10-second manual check —
search the business name, glance at their Facebook/Google listing, confirm
the email shown there matches. Takes a few seconds and avoids bouncing off a
made-up address or, worse, emailing a real stranger's inbox.

Results: 44/100 businesses had a candidate email (6 "High", 36 "Medium", 2
"Low" — treat all three as needing the check above). 56/100 had nothing
publicly findable — phone/text is still the best channel for those.
