What Is Backflow?

Backflow occurs when water flows in the opposite direction from where it is supposed to go. This can happen due to a sudden drop in water pressure, known as backsiphonage, or from increased downstream pressure, known as backpressure.

When that happens, contaminants from irrigation systems, fire sprinklers, boilers, garden hoses, industrial equipment, or other cross-connections can enter the clean water supply. That is why a functioning backflow prevention device is so important.

Backflow testing helps verify that your assembly is operating properly and can prevent contaminated water from entering your potable water system.

Why Backflow Testing Matters

Backflow testing is about more than checking a box. It plays an important role in protecting health, safety, and regulatory compliance.

Protect Your Water Supply

A failed backflow preventer can allow hazardous substances to enter clean water lines. Regular testing helps catch problems early and keeps your drinking water better protected.

Stay Compliant With Local Requirements

Many municipalities and water authorities require annual backflow testing for certain properties and plumbing systems. Keeping up with scheduled inspections can help you avoid notices, penalties, or service issues.

Identify Repairs Early

Testing can reveal worn components, valve failures, pressure problems, or other issues before they turn into bigger and more expensive repairs.

Support Safe Plumbing Systems

Backflow prevention is a key part of a safe, properly maintained plumbing system, especially for irrigation lines, commercial buildings, multi-family properties, and systems with elevated contamination risk.

Signs You May Need Backflow Testing or Service

You may need to schedule backflow testing services if:

  • Your property is due for its annual backflow test
  • You received a notice from your local water authority
  • You have an irrigation or sprinkler system

Even if there are no obvious signs of a problem, routine testing is the best way to confirm your device is working as intended.
