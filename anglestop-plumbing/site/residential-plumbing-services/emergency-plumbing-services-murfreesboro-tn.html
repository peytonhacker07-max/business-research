Common Plumbing Emergencies We Handle

At Angle Stop Plumbing, we respond to a wide range of urgent plumbing issues. Some of the most common plumbing emergencies include:

Burst Pipes

A burst pipe can release a large amount of water into your home in a short time. Fast action is critical to reduce structural damage, protect belongings, and restore water service.

Major Water Leaks

Hidden or visible leaks can damage floors, walls, cabinets, and ceilings. Emergency plumbing service helps stop the leak and prevent further water damage.

Overflowing Toilets

An overflowing toilet can quickly create a messy and unsanitary situation. We diagnose the cause and provide the repair needed to restore proper function.

Sewer Backups

Sewage backups are serious plumbing emergencies that can create health risks and property damage. Prompt service is essential for safety and cleanup.

No Hot Water

If your water heater fails unexpectedly, especially during colder weather or heavy household use, emergency service may be needed to restore hot water quickly.

Shut-Off Valve Failures

A broken or stuck shut-off valve can make it difficult to control a leak or isolate a plumbing issue. Repairing or replacing the valve can be an important part of emergency service.

Signs You Need Emergency Plumbing Services

Not every plumbing problem is an emergency, but some issues need immediate professional attention. You may need emergency plumbing services if you notice:

  • Water rapidly leaking or flooding inside your property
  • A burst or frozen pipe
  • Sewage backing up into drains, tubs, or toilets
  • A toilet overflowing and not stopping
  • No water supply in part or all of the property
  • No hot water due to sudden system failure
  • Strong sewage odors combined with drainage issues
  • Multiple drains backing up at the same time
  • Water damage spreading across walls, ceilings, or floors

If a plumbing problem is causing active damage, creating a safety concern, or disrupting essential water use, it should be treated as urgent.

What to Do During a Plumbing Emergency

If you are facing a plumbing emergency, taking a few quick steps can help reduce damage before professional help arrives:

  • Shut off the water supply if it is safe to do so.
  • Turn off the water heater if a major leak or burst pipe is involved.
  • Avoid using affected fixtures or drains to prevent worsening the problem.
  • Move valuables or furniture away from standing water if possible.
  • Contact Angle Stop Plumbing for emergency plumbing service.

Quick action can help limit damage while you wait for a professional plumber to assess and repair the issue.
