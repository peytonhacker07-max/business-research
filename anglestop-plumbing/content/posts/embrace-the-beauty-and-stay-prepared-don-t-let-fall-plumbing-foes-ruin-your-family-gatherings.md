### The Reminder in the Chill: Be Thankful, Be Vigilant 🙏

This season of Thanksgiving preparation is wonderful, but the sudden chill serves as a perfect reminder: don't get too comfortable! While we are thankful for a smoothly running home, we shouldn't forget the routine maintenance that keeps it that way.

Why is this so important right now? Because your plumbing system is about to go into high gear.

As you plan those big family gatherings—Thanksgiving dinner, holiday parties, or just hosting out-of-town guests—you’ll be putting a major strain on your home’s essential systems:

- More Showers: Your water heater will be working overtime.

- More Cooking: Your garbage disposal and kitchen drain will be processing more food waste.

- More Toilets: Your sewer lines and fixtures will be getting a workout.

### The Fall Plumbing Check-Up Checklist (For Peace of Mind)

A little proactive work now can prevent a major, stressful plumbing emergency when your house is full of loved ones. Nothing ruins a holiday faster than a backed-up sewer line or a water heater outage!

Here’s what Angle Stop Plumbing recommends focusing on this fall:

- Water Heater Tune-Up: Sediment build-up can drastically reduce efficiency. A flush and inspection ensures you have enough hot water for everyone and protects your unit's lifespan.

- Drain Cleaning & Inspection: We can inspect main lines for lurking clogs that the increased holiday traffic will surely expose. Better to clear out that slow drain now than on the morning of Thanksgiving.

- Exterior Winterization: Have us turn off and drain your exterior hose bibs to prevent freezing and bursting pipes when the temperature truly drops.

- Sump Pump Test: If you have a basement, ensure your sump pump is ready for any heavy rain or snowmelt.

This fall, embrace the spirit of gratitude, but let’s also be wise stewards of the home we’ve been blessed with. Schedule your routine maintenance with us today and ensure the only surprises your family gets are good ones!
