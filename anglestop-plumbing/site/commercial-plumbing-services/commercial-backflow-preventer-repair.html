Trusted Commercial Backflow Preventer Repair

A properly working backflow preventer is essential to protecting your potable water supply from contamination caused by backpressure or backsiphonage. When these assemblies wear out, leak, stick, or fail inspection, repairs need to be completed promptly and correctly.

Angle Stop Plumbing repairs commercial backflow devices for a wide range of properties, including:

  • Medical and dental offices
  • Schools and municipal buildings
  • Warehouses and industrial properties
  • Hotels and hospitality facilities

Our Commercial Backflow Repair Services

Angle Stop Plumbing offers complete service for malfunctioning or failed backflow assemblies.

Our commercial backflow preventer repair services include:

  • Diagnostic inspection to identify leaks, pressure problems, failed components, and performance issues
  • Repair of commercial backflow assemblies including common device types used in business and industrial settings
  • Replacement of worn internal components such as checks, seals, springs, relief valves, and shutoff parts
  • Post-repair testing to verify proper operation and compliance
  • Code-compliance support for properties that must meet local and municipal requirements
  • Scheduled maintenance recommendations to reduce future failures and prolong device life

Our goal is simple: restore the assembly, protect the water supply, and help your property remain compliant.

Signs You May Need Commercial Backflow Preventer Repair

If your property is showing any of the following issues, it may be time to schedule service:

  • Your assembly failed a required backflow test
  • Water is leaking from the relief valve or around the device
  • You notice pressure fluctuations in the plumbing system
  • The backflow preventer is making unusual noises
  • The device shows signs of corrosion, wear, or age
  • You received notice from the city, inspector, or water authority
  • Your building has had recent plumbing or irrigation issues

Ignoring a failing backflow preventer can lead to bigger plumbing problems, compliance issues, and potential health concerns. Prompt repair helps reduce risk and avoid interruptions to your business.
