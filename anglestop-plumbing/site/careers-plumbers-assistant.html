Start your plumbing career with Angle Stop Plumbing serving Murfreesboro and Middle Tennessee homes and businesses as a Plumber's Assistant. Begin a Career in Plumbing

A Plumber's Assistant plays an important role in helping licensed plumbers complete residential and commercial plumbing work safely, efficiently, and to a high standard. At Angle Stop Plumbing, this position is ideal for someone who wants to learn the trade from the ground up while working in the field every day.

This is more than an entry-level job. It is a chance to develop practical skills, gain direct experience with plumbing systems, and build a long-term career in a stable, in-demand industry.

What a Plumber's Assistant Does

As a Plumber's Assistant at Angle Stop Plumbing, you will support plumbers with a wide range of daily tasks, including:

  • Assisting with plumbing installations, repairs, and maintenance
  • Loading, unloading, and organizing tools, equipment, and materials
  • Preparing work areas and helping maintain a safe, clean job site
  • Digging, lifting, measuring, cutting, and assembling materials as directed
  • Helping diagnose plumbing issues under the guidance of a lead plumber
  • Cleaning up after jobs and ensuring equipment is properly stored
  • Environment can include crawlspace and attic work
  • Communicating professionally with team members and customers when needed

This role is hands-on and active, making it a good fit for someone who enjoys physical work, problem-solving, and learning by doing.

What We're Looking For

Angle Stop Plumbing is looking for candidates who are motivated, reliable, and ready to work as part of a team. Successful applicants for the Plumber's Assistant position typically have:

  • A strong work ethic and positive attitude
  • Willingness to learn plumbing tools, systems, and job site procedures
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • The ability to follow instructions carefully
  • Dependability and punctuality
  • Physical ability to lift materials, work in tight spaces, and stay active throughout the day
  • A valid clean driver's license

Previous construction, maintenance, or plumbing experience can be helpful, but it is not required. Candidates with the right attitude and willingness to learn are encouraged to apply.

Why Work at Angle Stop Plumbing

Joining Angle Stop Plumbing means becoming part of a company that values both quality workmanship and team development. A Plumber's Assistant can expect an environment where hard work is respected and growth is encouraged.

Depending on company needs and the specific position, benefits may include:

  • Competitive pay
  • On-the-job training
  • Opportunities for advancement
  • Steady work in an essential industry
  • Supportive team environment
  • Skill development with experienced plumbing professionals

For someone interested in entering the trades, this position can be the first step toward a rewarding plumbing career.

Opportunities for Growth

Many skilled plumbers begin as assistants. Working as a Plumber's Assistant at Angle Stop Plumbing can help you build the foundation needed for future advancement. Over time, you may gain experience in:

  • Plumbing tools and safety procedures
  • Water, drain, and sewer line systems
  • Fixture installation
  • Troubleshooting and repair techniques
  • Job site organization and customer service

This kind of field experience can open the door to greater responsibility and long-term career development in plumbing.

Who Should Apply

This opportunity may be a strong fit for people who are:

  • Starting a career in the skilled trades
  • Looking to transition into plumbing from general labor or construction
  • Interested in hands-on work with real growth potential
  • Ready to learn from experienced professionals
  • Committed to delivering quality service and dependable support

If you are looking for a practical career path with room to grow, the Plumber's Assistant role at Angle Stop Plumbing could be the right next step.
